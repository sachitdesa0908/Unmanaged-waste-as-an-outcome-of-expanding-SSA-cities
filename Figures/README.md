# Figures

## Overview
This directory contains the figures and spatial maps associated with the study **"Unmanaged waste as an outcome of expanding sub-Saharan African cities"**, published in *npj Urban Sustainability*.
The figures illustrate the geographical distribution of unmanaged waste sites, spatial patterns of waste risk, and key findings from the statistical analyses across the ten study cities.

## Directory structure
### 1. `Manuscript_figures/`
Contains the five main figures presented in the published manuscript (`Figure_1.png` to `Figure_5.png`).
These figures summarise the principal findings, including spatial distributions of unmanaged waste, relationships with urban characteristics, and results from the statistical analyses.

### 2. `Maps/`
Contains two sets of city-specific maps for all ten study cities:

- **Waste site maps (`City_v0.png`):** Display the geographical locations and relative sizes of mapped unmanaged waste sites in 2024, alongside urban features including built-up areas, green spaces, and water bodies.
- **Risk maps (`city_risk.png`):** Display the spatial distribution of modelled waste intensity and classified risk levels across each city, illustrating geographical variation in unmanaged waste risk.

Maps are provided for Abidjan, Accra, Dakar, Freetown, Johannesburg, Kampala, Kinshasa, Lagos, Maputo, and Nairobi.

## Related data and code
The underlying waste site locations, polygons, and urban covariates are available in the `Data/` directory. The analytical scripts supporting the spatial modelling and visualisations are provided in `Code/`.
For detailed descriptions and interpretations of the figures, refer to the associated manuscript and its Supplementary Information.
